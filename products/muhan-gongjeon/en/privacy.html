Privacy Policy

ENDLESS ORBIT does not require an account. Game records are stored on the device or through the distribution platform, and the operator processes only the information needed to provide support.

Effective date: September 1, 2026

1. Information processed and purposes

High scores, progress, lives, and language, sound, haptic, and notification settings generated on Android, iOS, or the web are stored on the device or through the distribution platform to preserve game progress and settings. They are not transmitted to or stored on the operator's own servers and remain until the app or site data is deleted. Operating-system or platform backups may remain after the app is deleted, so users should also review their device and platform settings.

When a user contacts support, the operator processes the sender's email address, inquiry, and attachments to respond and investigate errors. This information is retained for 90 days after the inquiry is closed and then deleted.

In the Apps in Toss mini app, Toss may process screen, button, and game events, app version, build and runtime information, and scores submitted to Game Center to operate the game, monitor quality, provide leaderboards, and deliver rewarded ads. Retention follows Toss policies and user settings.

In mobile builds that display ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, approximate location, and consent choices to deliver and measure ads, control frequency, prevent abuse, and manage consent. Retention follows Google policies and user settings.

2. External services

The operator does not sell personal information. Depending on the distribution environment, the following services may process necessary information under their own policies.

Google LLC (Gmail): Used to deliver and store support inquiries. Inquiry data is transmitted over the network and may be processed on servers outside Korea. The operator retains it for 90 days after the inquiry is closed. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Toss: Provides mini-app storage, usage analytics, server time, Game Center, and integrated advertising. The game automatically submits the score to Game Center when an Endless Mode run ends. See the Toss Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob: May process information needed for advertising, consent management, measurement, frequency control, and abuse prevention. Rewarded ads play only when the user chooses to watch them. Information may be processed on servers outside Korea. See How Google uses information from apps.

3. Deletion and user rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing for support records by emailing support. On Android and iOS, users can delete app data in device settings or uninstall the app. On the web, users can delete stored data and permissions for the game site in browser settings. Information in Toss mini-app storage and Game Center can be managed through Toss app settings or Toss support. Google advertising information and consent choices can be managed through Ad privacy choices in the game, device settings, or Google ad settings. Life-recovery notifications can be disabled in game settings. Because the game has no operator-managed account, there is no separate account deletion process. Records that must be retained by law are stored separately for the required period.

4. Security and children's privacy

Access to support inquiries is limited to the person responsible for handling them, and the records are deleted after the retention period. The game does not directly request a child's name or contact information. A parent or guardian may request access to or deletion of information concerning a child by emailing support.
