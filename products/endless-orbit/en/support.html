Customer Support

Support

If you have a problem with the game or a privacy request, contact us by email.

Support email

contact@akoracorp.com

Operator: AKORA Co., Ltd.

Information to include

  • Platform: Android, iPhone, or Apps in Toss
  • App version and device or operating-system version
  • The screen where the issue occurred and steps to reproduce it
  • If possible, a screenshot with personal information hidden

Do not send passwords, verification codes, complete advertising IDs, identity documents, or other sensitive information.

Background music

  1. Check the master sound and background-music volume in game settings.
  2. Close the app completely and open it again.
  3. If the problem continues after leaving and returning to the app, include the device model and operating-system version in your inquiry.

Restoring records

Game records are generally stored on the device or through the platform in use. Records may not be recoverable after the app or app data is deleted or after changing devices. The game currently has no operator-managed account or server synchronization.

Advertising rewards

A life is granted only after an ad has been watched to completion and the game receives a reward-completion signal. A reward may not be granted if no ad is available, the ad is closed early, or a network error occurs. If the problem repeats, include the time and platform in your inquiry. Lives also recover over time without ads.

Privacy requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing for support records held by the operator, use Privacy Request as the email subject. We will explain any identity-verification steps and the result as necessary.

Use the Android, iOS, or browser data-deletion settings to remove game records stored on the device. Information and advertising consent managed directly by Toss or Google can be controlled using the methods described in the Privacy Policy.
